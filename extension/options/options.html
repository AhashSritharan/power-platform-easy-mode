General

Floating launcher
A small switch tab on the right edge of Dynamics 365 and Power Platform pages that opens the command palette. Drag it up or down to move it; hover it and click ✕ to hide it.

🔒 Privacy: this extension collects nothing. All settings and tool data are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and never transmitted anywhere. The only network requests are the ones tools make to the sites they integrate with (your Dynamics / Power Platform environments, Jira, and — if you configure it — the Clockify API).
